AI for building drawings

From hand drawing to CAD file

rebar uses artificial intelligence to turn hand-drawn and scanned building drawings into layered, accurately scaled CAD files. For public authorities, architects, engineers and contractors working with existing buildings.

Move your mouse over the page to see it drawn up in CAD.

Tap “Show as CAD” to see the page drawn up in CAD.

How it works

  1. 1

    Upload the drawing

    Scan, photo or PDF. Yellowed tracing paper, pencil notes, stamps and corrections included.

  2. 2

    The AI reads the plan

    Walls, doors, windows, stairs, dimensions and room names are recognised, straightened and set to the correct scale.

  3. 3

    Download the CAD file

    Export as DWG or DXF with organised layers – ready to continue in your CAD software.

Who it's for

Public authorities

Make the building permit archive digital and reusable. Historic drawings become data that can be shared, searched and used in new cases.

Architects and engineers

Start renovations and conversions from a digital basis instead of redrawing existing conditions from scratch.

Contractors

Get accurately scaled plans fast for tenders, quantity take-offs and site planning.

What it recognises

rebar understands the elements of a drawing – not just the lines – and puts each on its own layer.

  • WallsA-WALL
  • DoorsA-DOOR
  • WindowsA-GLAZ
  • StairsA-FLOR-STRS
  • DimensionsA-ANNO-DIMS
  • Room names and textA-ANNO-TEXT
